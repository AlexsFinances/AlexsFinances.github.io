Welcome

Finance Navigator is an educational resource designed to help students and lifelong learners understand the fundamentals of economics, investing and personal finance. Whether you’re preparing for college applications, planning your first investment or just curious about how money works, this site offers detailed explanations, interactive tools and practical insights to guide you.

Our Mission

Finance Navigator exists to make financial education accessible and engaging for everyone. Too often money seems intimidating or exclusive. By combining clear explanations, real‑world examples and interactive tools we aim to build confidence and curiosity around budgeting, borrowing, investing and the economy.

What You’ll Find

Economics Basics

Learn how scarcity, opportunity cost, supply and demand, gross domestic product (GDP), inflation and unemployment shape our world. Understand how monetary and fiscal policy influence economic growth and stability.

Investing Fundamentals

Explore why people invest, the trade‑off between risk and reward, and how to build a diversified portfolio. Discover the differences between stocks, bonds, index funds, ETFs and mutual funds, and see how asset allocation changes with your goals and time horizon.

Personal Finance

Get practical advice on budgeting, saving, credit scores, student loans, retirement accounts and more. Learn how compound interest grows your savings and why staying ahead of inflation is so important.

Interactive Tools

Our calculators help you build a budget, calculate compound interest, simulate credit card payoff and estimate student loan payments. Use them to plan and make informed decisions.
